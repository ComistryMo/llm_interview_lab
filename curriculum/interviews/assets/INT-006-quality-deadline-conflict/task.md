# INT-006 · Resolve a Quality versus Deadline Conflict

## Scenario

Describe a real or clearly labeled hypothetical situation where a deadline conflicted with an important quality, reliability, privacy, or safety concern.

## Primary question

Explain your responsibility, the evidence available at the time, how you communicated the trade-off, the decision, the outcome, and what you changed afterward.

## Constraints

- Do not disclose confidential employer or customer information.
- Separate your own contribution from the team's work.
- Do not invent metrics; state when an outcome was not measured.

## Follow-up axes

The interviewer may ask what you would do with less authority, worse evidence, or a repeated disagreement.
