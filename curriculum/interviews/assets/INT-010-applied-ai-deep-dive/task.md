# INT-010 · Deep Dive an Applied AI Delivery

## Scenario

Choose one owned and suitably sanitized AI application project. If no such project exists, use an explicitly hypothetical system and say so.

## Primary question

Explain the user need, your exact ownership, baseline, architecture, evaluation, key trade-off, failed attempt, production evidence, and what you would change now.

## Constraints

- Do not reveal confidential code, data, names, configurations, or metrics.
- Separate team output from personal decisions and implementation.
- Mark unavailable evidence as unavailable rather than estimating it.

## Follow-up axes

The interviewer may challenge causality, baseline fairness, reliability, cost, or the scope of your contribution.
