# INT-021 · Deep Dive a Platform Migration

## Scenario

Choose one owned and sanitized platform or pipeline migration. If you do not have one, use an explicitly hypothetical migration and label every assumption.

## Primary question

Explain the original constraint, your ownership, alternatives, compatibility plan, data and artifact correctness, staged migration, failure or rollback, measured outcome, and remaining debt.

## Constraints

- Do not disclose proprietary topology, credentials, customer data, or internal metrics.
- Separate team ownership from your contribution.
- Explain how correctness was verified, not just that jobs completed.

## Follow-up axes

The interviewer may challenge dual-write consistency, rollback, version skew, or claimed cost savings.
