# INT-001 · Frame an AI Support Assistant

## Scenario

A fictional subscription software product receives many support requests. A team proposes an AI assistant, but it has not yet verified which requests cause the most user pain or which actions may be automated safely.

## Primary question

Frame the first product iteration. Explain the user problem, why AI may or may not be appropriate, the smallest useful workflow, success and guardrail metrics, failure handling, and what evidence you would collect before launch.

## Constraints

- Do not invent user research, ticket volume, model accuracy, or business impact.
- Distinguish assumptions from known facts and say how each important assumption will be tested.
- Include a path for escalation to a person.

## Follow-up axes

The interviewer may change the risk level, budget, user segment, or available labeled data. Revise the proposal rather than defending the original plan automatically.
